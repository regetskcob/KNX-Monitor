    What should your home be able to do?

    The home builder package starts with your everyday life and leads through room book and floor plan to clear documents for your electrician. A check on the finished bus is planned for later.

    02 — Wishes

    You decide on moments, the technology follows.

    Planning KNX shouldn’t require knowing actuator channels. Play through a few moments. Below you see what’s behind them and what already belongs in the shell.

    03 — Floor plan

    On the plan you see whether it works in everyday life.

    Upload a floor plan, place devices, and you see how they sit against doors, furniture and paths. Lights are switchable, dimmable, tunable or coloured, sockets have permanent power, can be switched or also measure current, windows get a roller shutter or a venetian blind with slats. The plan works out the channels from that. Try it: Tap a device and change its type.

    04 — With your electrician

    Documents your electrician can work with.

    You go through the plan with your electrician and hand over a clean list. Coordination runs through these documents, not in the app.

    Room book
    Wishes per room, priority and the type of every light and socket
    Channels
    Switching, dimming, blinds and heating, each with reserve. Your contractor decides on actuators and the distribution board
    Without Plus
    Wishes with eight moments, room book, one level with floor plan, channel count, PDF without dimensions, CSV and the home plan file
    With Plus
    More levels and outdoor area, checks, weather rules, deadlines by type of construction, quote check, PDF with dimensions and DXF
    Deadlines
    What should be settled before pouring concrete, rough-in and plastering, matched to your type of construction
    05 — Planned

    Later the floor plan should become a view of your bus.

    This isn’t built yet. Devices from the plan are meant to get their group addresses, so you see states and telegrams where they happen in the house. This is planned without the package too, with any floor plan of your own.

    07 — Questions

    Frequently asked questions

    Does the package replace my electrician or planner?

    It helps you clarify your wishes and hand them over cleanly. Electrical installation, actuators, distribution board and protection stay with your contractor, lighting design with a lighting designer.

    Does the app tell me what KNX costs?

    It shows what can be calculated from the plan: channels, devices and reserve. That lets you compare quotes on the same basis. Prices come from your contractor’s quote.

    Where do my floor plan and wishes live?

    On your device. You can pass them on as a file, yourself and only if you want to.

    Do I need a finished KNX installation?

    No, planning works without a bus and without ETS.

    When will it be available?

    The first stage comes with the apps, which are still in testing. So we aren’t naming a date. News on Instagram.
