KNX Monitor · Privacy policy

Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026

In short: We collect as little as possible: no account needed to use the app, no analytics, no tracking. Everything the app processes about your installation stays on your device or your KNX box. Exceptions: if you buy Plus or Pro, our service providers RevenueCat (and on Windows Paddle) process the purchase data; if you choose to create an account, we store your email address and your devices at Supabase in Frankfurt; and on Android the QR scanner (Google ML Kit) sends technical diagnostic data to Google, see below.

Controller

The controller within the meaning of the GDPR is the provider named in the legal notice.

The app (iPhone, iPad, Android, desktop)

No data collection by us

The app does not send any data to us unless you create an account. It contains no advertising or tracking libraries and needs no account to be used; only a purchase on Windows requires one. For the optional account we use Supabase (see “Account”); we run no other servers for the app.

What is stored locally on your device

  • Connection settings of your KNX interface (IP address, port)
  • Data read from your ETS project or CSV export: group addresses, names, datapoint types, devices, rooms, links
  • Captured telegrams, recordings, long-term history, check results (health, commissioning) and your notes
  • App settings such as language, favourites and notification rules
  • With KNX Secure: the keys and tunnel passwords read from the keyring, so the app can reconnect and decrypt telegrams. They are kept in the system keychain (iOS/macOS Keychain, Android Keystore, Windows or Linux keyring), on this device only. A tunnel password entered by hand is not stored.

The password of a protected ETS project and the keyring password are only used to decrypt them during import and are not stored. You can delete all data in the app at any time or uninstall the app, which removes the data from the device.

Network access

The app only accesses your local network to communicate via KNXnet/IP with the KNX interface or IP router you choose and to discover interfaces on the network. iOS asks for the “Local Network” permission for this. The app itself does not communicate with the internet; the exceptions are purchases (see “In-app purchases”), the optional account (see “Account”) and the QR scanner on Android (see “Camera and QR code”).

Camera and QR code (KNX box)

The camera is only used when you add a KNX box in the app, to read the QR code on its label. The camera image is analysed on the device and is neither stored nor transmitted. The QR code contains the Wi-Fi credentials and the box password; with them the app joins the box’s Wi-Fi and pairs. Afterwards only the device access and the box’s Wi-Fi are stored in the system keychain, not the box password.

On Android, Google’s ML Kit recognises the QR code. ML Kit works on the device but sends technical diagnostic data to Google (for example device model, operating system, app version, performance and error data and a per-installation identifier) so that Google can run and improve the service. The camera image is not part of it. Details: ML Kit data disclosure and Google’s privacy policy. On iOS, iPadOS and macOS the system itself (Apple Vision) recognises the code; no data is transmitted.

KNX box

When you connect the app to a KNX box, it also puts your ETS project, your own datapoint types and the keyring on the box and reads its history. The box is your own device on the local network; the data does not leave it.

Notifications

Alarm notifications are created locally on the device if you allow them. No server push service is used.

Export and sharing

Reports (PDF), CSV and ETS exports are only created when you trigger them. Where they go is up to you, via your operating system’s share sheet.

Account (optional)

You only need an account if a purchase should work on several devices or if you buy on Windows. It runs on Supabase (Supabase, Inc.; servers in Frankfurt am Main, EU). Stored are: your email address (with “Sign in with Apple” possibly Apple’s relay address), a random account ID, your devices (device name as chosen or reported by the system, platform, app version, last seen) and your plan (Free, Plus, Pro with expiry date). Project, telegrams, keyring, recordings and watchers are never stored in the account. To sign in we email you a code, or you sign in with Apple or Google. In that case Apple or Google passes your email address, an identifier for your account there and, where you have set it there, your name (Google also the address of your profile picture) to Supabase, which stores them with the account. We never see your password. If you add a passkey, we store its public key and a label for the device or password manager (e.g. “iCloud Keychain”); the private key never leaves your device or password manager. The privacy policies of Apple and Google apply as well. The legal basis is the performance of the contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). You can delete your account in the app at any time (Plus and Pro → Account → Delete account); this deletes all data named above at Supabase and unlinks your purchases at RevenueCat. Details: Supabase privacy policy.

Buying on Windows (Paddle)

On Windows you buy through our checkout page at Paddle (Paddle.com Market Ltd., United Kingdom). Paddle is the merchant of record: Paddle processes your name, email address, payment and billing data, pays the VAT and issues the invoice; we only receive the account ID and which product was bought. Details: Paddle privacy policy.

In-app purchases (Plus and Pro)

You buy Plus and Pro through the App Store or Google Play; payment and payment details stay with Apple or Google. So that the app knows what you bought, we use the service RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc., USA). For this the app sends a random, anonymous identifier, the store’s purchase receipts and technical details (e.g. app version, operating system, language, IP address) to RevenueCat. Your name, email address or data about your KNX installation are not included. This only happens when you open the “Plus and Pro” page, buy or restore something, and when the app starts, to check your plan. The legal basis is the performance of the purchase contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Data may be transferred to the USA. Details: RevenueCat privacy policy. On Windows you buy through Paddle (see below); there are no purchases on Linux and in the add-on, so none of this happens there.

App Store and Google Play

Downloading the app is subject to the privacy policies of Apple or Google. If you have agreed to it in your device settings, Apple or Google provide us with aggregated, non-personal statistics (e.g. install counts, crash reports). We have no influence on this and cannot identify individuals from it.

The Home Assistant add-on

The add-on runs on your own Home Assistant host. All data (project, Secure keys, telegrams, long-term history, recordings, settings) is kept in the add-on’s data folder on that host, is part of your Home Assistant backups (protect them with a backup encryption key) and is removed when you uninstall the add-on. The add-on records the bus continuously while it is running. Access goes through the Home Assistant interface (ingress) and is authenticated by Home Assistant. For notifications and for reading the KNX integration, the add-on uses the Home Assistant API on the same host. It does not send any data to us or to third parties.

This website

This website is served by GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When you visit the site, GitHub processes technically necessary data, in particular your IP address, date and time of the request and browser information, and stores it in server logs for security purposes. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in providing the website securely and reliably (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Data may be transferred to the USA; GitHub is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. See GitHub’s privacy statement for details.

No cookies, no tracking, no third-party content

This website sets no cookies, uses no analytics or tracking tools and embeds no third-party content. The fonts (Literata, Mona Sans, Monaspace and Material Symbols) are served by this website itself, not by Google Fonts or any other provider.

Support via GitHub Issues

Support and feedback go through GitHub Issues. An issue is publicly visible and requires a GitHub account; processing there is governed by GitHub’s privacy statement. We use what you write in an issue only to handle your request (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR). Please don’t post confidential data such as passwords or your ETS project there.

Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection (Art. 15–21 GDPR), and the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. Since the app itself does not send any data to us, we hold no data about your use of the app.
