KNX Monitor
Bus monitor & diagnostics for KNX

See what happens on your KNX bus.

Every telegram, made sense of with your ETS project: devices, rooms and values instead of columns of hex. Plus a check of your installation for silent sensors, wrong datapoint types and bus problems.

As a Home Assistant add-on

Coming soon for iPhone, iPad, Android, Mac, Windows and Linux. Your installation data never leaves your network.

Live · demo houseLast 60 s

    What the add-on does today
    02 — In the add-on today

    Bus monitor and diagnostics, in Home Assistant today.

    Free, in the sidebar, on every device with Home Assistant, on the go through the Home Assistant app too. The add-on stays connected and keeps recording, even with no browser open.

    Telegrams
    Live with source, destination and value; search, filter, read and write values
    ETS project
    .knxproj up to ETS6, password-protected too: names, datapoint types, devices and rooms
    Overview
    Every group address with its value and a mini history, by rooms and functions
    Objects
    Actuators as DIN rail devices with channel LEDs, like above
    Health
    Silent devices, cycles, datapoint types and bus quality, with a PDF report
    History
    Continuous recording day by day, recordings with name and note
    Experts
    Bus monitor with raw frames, topology, comparison with the ETS project, commissioning checklist
    Connection
    Tunneling, routing and KNX IP Secure
    03 — Coming as an app

    Soon without Home Assistant too, right at the gateway.

    For the building site, the distribution board and the office next to ETS: the same tools as an app on whatever device is in your hand.

    Mobile
    iPhone, iPad and Android
    Desktop
    Mac, Windows and Linux
    Connection
    Directly to your IP interface or IP router
    On site
    ETS project and recordings live on the device, no internet needed
