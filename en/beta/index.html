KNX Monitor · Beta

Beta

The apps are built and currently in beta. Anyone can join, and every feature is free, including those of Plus and Pro. To test you need a KNX IP interface or IP router; without an installation you can try everything in the demo house.

iPhone, iPad and Mac

  1. Install Apple’s TestFlight app.
  2. Open the beta link on the device and tap “Accept”.
  3. Tap “Install” in TestFlight. New versions arrive there automatically.

Requires iOS or iPadOS 15 or later; on the Mac, macOS 13.5 or later.

Android

  1. Join the Google group with your Google account. Only then does Google Play let you into the test.
  2. Join the test and choose “Become a tester”.
  3. Install KNX Monitor from Google Play through the link on the test page. Updates arrive through Google Play as usual.

Requires Android 6 or later. It sometimes takes a few minutes until Google Play knows you joined.

The thank-you

90 days of Pro, free. When KNX Monitor launches on the App Store or Google Play, the thank-you starts on the first launch of the store version: 90 days of Pro on the device you used the beta on. You don’t have to do or sign up for anything; afterwards the app carries on with Free until you choose Plus or Pro yourself. The app shows you until when it applies.

Feedback

We welcome bugs and wishes: through TestFlight feedback (take a screenshot and share it), in the GitHub issues or to support@knx-monitor.de. If it is about your installation, the diagnostics package helps.
